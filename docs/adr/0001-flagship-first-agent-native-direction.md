# Flagship-first, agent-native direction

Laugh Tale 1.0 positioned itself as "AI-generated sites first, with a small headless layer", and the theme work that followed produced four presentation worlds without touching the packages. Placing generated output beside the hand-built benchmark (`tokyo-trip-map`) showed that the quality gap sits in interaction feel and map/list choreography, not in themes.

We decided that a single flagship site, generated from real itinerary text, is the product's source of truth. The Eternal Pose generator exists to reproduce the flagship's quality for new trips; the headless packages exist to make that reproduction reliable; publishing them as a general-purpose headless library is a by-product, not the goal. APIs are designed agent-first and human-compatible: shaped for reliable reproduction by an agent, usable by a React developer.

**North star.** A site generated by Eternal Pose from the Tokyo itinerary, depending only on published packages, must not read as lower quality than the benchmark when the two are placed side by side. When trading off, the order is interaction feel, then visual craft, then map behaviour.

## Considered options

- **General headless primitives library** (the Radix / React Aria path). Rejected: headless is a commodity and we have no differentiation there.
- **Generator only, packages as an invisible implementation detail.** Rejected: without a proven flagship the quality claim is a promise.
- **Flagship feeds generator, headless as by-product.** Chosen.

## Consequences

- The number of recipes becomes an outcome, not a target. Every recipe must pass the Tokyo acceptance (ADR-0005) to exist. This supersedes the "six authored worlds" target in the 2026-08-27 catalog spec and the "four distinctive worlds" target in the 2026-08-30 theme spec.
- The `feat/distinctive-theme-gallery` branch and the unmerged recipe worktrees are paused, neither merged nor deleted, until the flagship passes.
